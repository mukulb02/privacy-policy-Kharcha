Privacy Policy
Effective 5 October 2026 · Developer: Mukul

In short
Kharcha reads bank SMS and payment-app notifications on your phone to log your spending.
Everything it records stays on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers.
Nothing is uploaded, sold or shared with anyone.
You can switch capture off, or delete all data, at any time in Settings.
What Kharcha is
Kharcha is a personal budget and spending tracker for Android. Its main feature is recording your spends automatically from the transaction alerts your bank and payment apps already send you, so you don't have to type in every purchase.

Information the app reads
SMS messages (READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS). When a new SMS arrives, Kharcha checks whether it is a bank or UPI transaction alert. From those it takes the amount, whether money was debited or credited, the merchant or payee name, the last four digits of the account or card, the transaction reference number and the payment mode. With your consent, it can also read past messages once to import earlier transactions. Messages that are not transaction alerts, such as personal messages and OTPs, are skipped and not stored.
Notifications from payment and banking apps (notification access). If you turn on notification access, Kharcha reads notifications from a fixed list of payment, banking and messaging apps, such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and your bank's app, to catch payment confirmations. Notifications from all other apps are ignored.
Information you enter. Spends you add or edit by hand, notes, categories and budgets.
How it is used
This information is used only to show you your spending: the transaction list, categories, budgets, charts, and alerts such as "₹450 · Swiggy, ₹8,200 left this month". It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit scoring or any other purpose.

Where it is stored
All data is kept in a private database inside the app on your phone. Kharcha has no servers and no user accounts. It does not upload your data, and the app's backups are switched off, so the data is not copied to cloud backups either.

Sharing
Kharcha does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone, including the developer. It contains no advertising or analytics code.

Your choices
Turn bank SMS capture or payment-notification capture on or off in Settings inside the app.
Remove the SMS permission or notification access at any time in your phone's settings.
Edit or delete any individual transaction in the app.
Delete everything with Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data.
Notifications
If you allow it, Kharcha shows a notification after each recorded spend and warns you when you get close to a budget limit. You can turn these alerts off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Security
Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox, so other apps cannot read it. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open Kharcha can see your spends, so keep your phone locked.

Children
Kharcha is meant for adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
